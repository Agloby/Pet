# Shark look mechanics
The shark is a flexible soft toy, with no separate neck. Keep the rear torso and tail root anchored with stable baseline and scale. The front third gently bends and turns, the muzzle leads gaze, and the original black eyes turn with their physical head surfaces. Attached pectoral fins follow gently. Do not rotate the complete shark or replace its eyes. For up/down use raised/lowered snout with readable eyelid and eye direction, preserve recognizable face. For left/right turn anterior head smoothly to viewer-left/viewer-right with foreshortening while rear body remains anchored. Maintain grey/white boundary, gill slits, dorsal fin, tail, teeth and compact silhouette; no props or extra parts.
